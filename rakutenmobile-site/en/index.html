A simple guide for switching or getting a new mobile plan in Japan

Check the important steps from opening the referral link to activating your service.

Campaign benefits, eligibility and deadlines may change. Always confirm the latest details on the official Rakuten Mobile page.

HOW TO APPLY

Three steps

  1. 01

    Open the referral link

    Continue to the official campaign page and sign in with your Rakuten ID.

  2. 02

    Check the terms and apply

    Choose number portability (MNP) or a new number, then check the current plan and deadline requirements.

  3. 03

    Complete activation requirements

    Complete all official requirements, which may include activation by a deadline and use of Rakuten Link.

BEFORE YOU START

Prepare these items

  • Rakuten ID and password
  • Current carrier details for MNP
  • Identity document and payment method
  • Latest campaign terms on the official page
